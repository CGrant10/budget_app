Wallet cards × the skins

The skins never bring their own markup to this page — they restyle it in place, and the established pattern is background: transparent, a hairline border, the skin's corner radius and its numerals (that is literally what body.skinned .acct-nw2 does today). Applied to a wallet card that strips the one thing the card is: its colour fill. The update button is kept on the page in all of these.

Left to right: the saturated cards as they would look on a standard theme, then the same cards under each skin — first with the skins' existing flat pattern, then with a thin colour edge kept so the type is still scannable. Compare the two skin columns; that is the decision.
