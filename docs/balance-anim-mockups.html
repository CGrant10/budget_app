Balance-first transaction animations

Six variations on the one you liked — all of them lead with what your balance is now rather than with the transaction. 00 is the original for comparison. Each row shows expense, income and transfer.

Note the transfer column. A transfer doesn't change your total balance, so a balance-first animation has nothing to report unless it shows the two accounts separately — each concept below handles that differently, and it's the main thing to judge them on.
