Direction
Budget app · design exploration

Three clean directions

Same screens, three restrained treatments — an alternative to the Glitch and HUD skins. Each one is a complete token set (color, type, spacing, radius, borders), so whichever you pick drops into style.css as a theme rather than a rewrite.

01

Ledger

editorial · warm light · serif numerals

Reads like a well-set financial statement. No cards and no shadows — structure comes entirely from hairline rules and whitespace. Serif numerals give the money weight and authority; the UI text stays a neutral sans so nothing competes with the figures. Feels considered and expensive, and it's the most legible of the three in daylight.

  • bg #FBFAF7
  • ink #14150F
  • accent #1F4D3D
  • serif tabular numerals
  • 0px radius · rules not boxes
  • 22px gutters
02

Quiet

soft dark · elevated cards · single accent

The safest modern default and the closest to your current dark app. Near-black ground, cards lifted a step with a 1px translucent edge, generous 18px radii, and exactly one accent (mint) reserved for positive money and the active nav item. Everything else is grayscale, so a single green number carries real signal.

  • bg #0E1013
  • card #16191D
  • accent #5ECF9E
  • geometric sans · tnum
  • 18px radius
  • one accent, strict
03

Grid

swiss · monospace data · structural

The most opinionated. Pure white, black rules, 10px letterspaced caps for labels, and every figure in monospace so columns align perfectly down the screen. Denser than the other two — more data per screen — and color is purely semantic (green in, red out, nothing decorative). Sharpest look, least forgiving of sloppy content.

  • bg #FFFFFF
  • rule #0A0B0C
  • semantic color only
  • monospace figures
  • 3px radius · full-bleed rules
  • dense rhythm
