Accounts hero × wallet cards

Flat wallet cards in the Ledger skin, with three ways to keep the mascot — or your own photo — on the page. Each treatment is shown twice: once with the cut-out Doberman, once with a photo, because they behave differently. A cut-out has transparent margins and wants to sit loose; a photo is a filled rectangle and needs cropping or it reads as a stray image. The version and update button are on the page in all six.

The photo here is a generated stand-in, not a real one — it is only there to show how a filled rectangular image sits in each layout.
