Weekly Planner under the skins

Measured from the live page in the Quiet skin. The problem is not spacing alone — it is that five money figures stack with nothing to tell them apart: the balance appears twice ($6,084.00 at the top and again in the summary cards), and "This month" and "Per week" show the same number under different labels whenever one week is left. Four of the cards are visually identical: same background, same border, 11px label over a 20px value. And the whole middle section has no container and no heading, so scrolling gives you no idea which part you are in.
